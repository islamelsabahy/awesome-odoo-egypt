# Contributing

This list stays useful only if every entry is real and checked.

1. **Open the link yourself** and confirm it works and says what you claim.
2. Add it to the right table with a factual one-line note. Say what you confirmed, and mark anything you could not verify (for example "body not reviewed").
3. Keep official sources (Odoo, ETA, OCA) separate from third-party or commercial ones.
4. No affiliate links, no unverifiable "best of" claims, no private or customer data.
5. Prefer English for the table notes. Arabic explanations are welcome in the checklists.

Found a dead or outdated link? Open an issue with the link and what you saw.
